Article 26 CRR:
Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital consists of paid-in capital, retained earnings, and disclosed reserves.

Article 51 CRR:
Additional Tier 1 (AT1) capital consists of instruments that are perpetual, subordinated, and fully paid-in.

Article 62 CRR:
Tier 2 capital consists of subordinated instruments with an original maturity of at least five years.

Article 72 CRR:
Total Own Funds is the sum of Tier 1 capital and Tier 2 capital.

COREP Own Funds Template C 01.00:
Row 010 reports Common Equity Tier 1 capital.
Row 020 reports Additional Tier 1 capital.
Row 040 reports Tier 2 capital.
Row 030 reports Total Own Funds.
